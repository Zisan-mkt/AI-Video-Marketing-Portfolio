AI Video Marketing Portfolio

Welcome to my AI Video Marketing Portfolio.

This repository showcases a collection of corporate and product promotional videos created using AI-assisted video production workflows, primarily with Google Flow.

My goal is to combine AI video generation, creative direction, prompt engineering, and marketing strategy to develop engaging visual content for business communication, brand awareness, and product promotion.

About This Portfolio

This portfolio features 6–10 AI-generated video projects developed for corporate and product marketing purposes.

Each project demonstrates different aspects of AI-assisted video production, including concept development, scriptwriting, scene planning, visual storytelling, and prompt engineering.
